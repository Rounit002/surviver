# Brief

## Intent

The user asked for a video that does not feel like an ad but converts founders into $29 entrants, built from
`D:/SurvierLOL/reports/Video ad buying psychology.md`. They set no duration, and asked for one video for
Instagram, YouTube, X and Facebook if possible. Use "400K monthly views" and never 13.4K. Use the ElevenLabs
voice and premium sound effects.

Concept: relatable humour hook ("You launched your SaaS… and twelve people saw it"), a silent beat with a
cricket, then the music drops in for the turn to "400,000 views a month". After that: the live stream
mechanism, the audience (devs, founders, students who use AI tools), no bidding and real clicks setting the
rank (peak: an example product climbs to #1), and an end card with $29 · 32 spots · surviver.lol.
